## About this source

A snapshot of the front page of upvote.at, an AT Protocol link aggregator, as fetched on the collection date. It is a ranked list of 30 submitted links with vote counts, source domains, blurbs and relative ages (for example 2 weeks ago, 1 month ago). It is an aggregator listing, not an article; the content belongs to the linked authors. The site describes itself as atmospheric upvotes where upvoting a link shares it and every upvote is a record in your own PDS. No site-level license statement was found in the page.

## Key facts

- The service describes itself as an AT Protocol native link aggregator: each upvote is a record stored in the voter's own PDS (this is the site's own blurb for its entry, item 13).
- The list mixes atproto ecosystem posts with unrelated tech news. Links may be web URLs or `at://` URIs of `site.standard.document` records (long-form posts published on atproto platforms such as Leaflet); each entry has a comments link and some link to author profiles by handle.
- Items relevant to permissioned data and groups on atproto: Reintroducing Spaces by daniel holmgren (dholms.leaflet.pub), described as zooming out and re-motivating the design of spaces, the new primitive for non-public data on atproto; What is a Group Host? by bryan newbold (bnewbold.leaflet.pub), describing a hosting pattern for Atmospheric Group accounts including group creation and app authorization flows; Give me some space! by Sri, part 1, saying Taproot can explore atproto spaces now (alpha).
- Other ecosystem items: vlpds docs overview (an atproto PDS whose only durable storage is an object store); a post on building an atproto link aggregator in Elixir by johanna (jola.dev); the AtmosphereConf 2027 announcement for Amsterdam (April 29 - May 2); chrisshank.com/at-tags on Tangled (a community proposal for mapping web pages back to AT URIs); at.new (creating atproto records and sharing drafts); Lexidraw, an Excalidraw fork that shares publicly with the Atmosphere or privately with iroh; plyr.fm, an audio streaming app; A Social Filesystem on overreacted.io (blurb: Formats over apps); an arXiv paper on feed-recommendation middleware (arXiv 2609.12958); and What comes after git by East River Source Control.
