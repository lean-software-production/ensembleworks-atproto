## Key facts

- Proposal status cited: bluesky-social/proposals#94, landing as `0016-permissioned-data`, was merged to proposals main on July 3, 2026. Core concepts relied on: spaces as permissioned repos with an owner DID, a `policy` field, member lists, bot or delegate DIDs writing on a user's behalf, and short-lived space credentials.
- Alpha: the issue says an alpha shipped on 2026-08-18 (`@atproto/api` and `@atproto/oauth-scopes` builds with version `0.0.0-spaces-alpha-20260818163953` on the `alpha` dist-tag) plus an invite-gated alpha PDS. The Spaces announcement is dated 2026-08-20 in the comments. The alpha PDS is to be deleted at the end of the alpha and there is no path from an alpha account to a real one.
- On 2026-08-29 the author recorded that `latest` of `@atproto/api` was `0.20.42` with no space NSIDs, that atproto#5187 (Atproto spaces) was still a draft with 104 commits and 198 files, and that atproto#5423 targets the `permissioned-data` branch.
- API surface verified against the alpha: `com.atproto.space.*` had 19 methods including `createRecord`, `applyWrites`, `getSpaceCredential`, `getDelegationToken`, `listRecords`; `com.atproto.simplespace.*` had `createSpace`, `addMember`, `removeMember`, `listMembers`, `checkUserAccess`, `getSpace`, `updateSpace`, `deleteSpace`.
- `createSpace` takes `type` (an NSID for the space modality, for example `app.bsky.group`), optional `skey`, `policy`, and `appAccess` (`#open` or `#allowList` of OAuth client IDs). Policy variants: `#publicPolicy`, `#memberListPolicy`, `#managingAppPolicy`.
- OAuth scope syntax as found: `space:<type>?authority=<did|self|*>&skey=<key|*>&collection=<nsid>&action=<...>&manage=<...>`. Actions are `read_self`, `read`, `create`, `update`, `delete`; manage operations are `create`, `update`, `delete`. `manage` and `action`/`collection` are mutually exclusive, so writing and provisioning are separate grants.
- Anonymity constraint: `com.atproto.space.defs#signedCommit` carries a signature by the author's atproto signing key, so an asker writing directly would expose their DID; a bot as sole writer is therefore structural.
- `com.atproto.space.createRecord` writes into the writer's own repo, so a bot-run PDS still holds every question until a login-time drain; the author corrected an earlier claim of no data at rest.
- Rate limit finding: writes share one bucket keyed on the writer DID at 5000 points per hour and 35000 per day with creates costing 3, giving roughly 1,666 questions per hour; self-hosting the PDS removes the ceiling.
- Credential and policy behavior read from the PDS branch: credentials live 7200s and are not single use; an unreachable managing app fails closed; the member list is a plain actor-store table, not signed into space state.
- Decision 2026-08-31: use `#memberListPolicy` rather than `#managingAppPolicy`, because a managing-app policy makes record readability depend on the app staying online.
- Space deletion as observed: the space row stays as a tombstone, the authority's repo is deleted, other members' records become unreadable, and `notifySpaceDeleted` is best-effort.
- Related calls noted: `com.atproto.space.registerNotify`, `notifyWrite`, `unregisterNotify` as a push path.
- Decision 2026-10-17: the recipient owns the space; the bot is added as its one member and acts as a temporary passthrough; the login drain moves records into the recipient's repo. Open items then: `appAccess` and the shared write rate limit.
- App-specific decisions (2026-08-29): migrate old records on login, write then delete, `tid` as idempotency key; record length 500 characters; central table dropped on an announced cutoff date. On 2026-09-13 the kill switch idea was dropped.
- Open question from tunjid-type concerns is not in this issue; see the proposals#94 notes for sync questions.
